NN Verification · Chapter 3 · companion to Albarghouthi (2021) §3.1–§3.3.

Albarghouthi's specification language wraps the network in a Hoare triple: $\{P\}\;\vec r \gets f(\vec x)\;\{Q\}$, read “for any input satisfying $P$, the output satisfies $Q$.” The hardest part of verification is not deciding whether such a triple holds — it is writing $P$ and $Q$ correctly in the first place. Below, you will translate three English properties about our canonical 2-2-1 ReLU toy network into formal triples by picking precondition and postcondition predicates from a palette, then watch the site decide whether your spec holds (or show you a counterexample if it doesn't).
