The DPLL(T) loop, step by step

NN Verification · Chapter 6 · companion to Albarghouthi (2021) §6.2–§6.3.

DPLL(T) decides satisfiability of an LRA formula by iterating Boolean abstraction → DPLL → theory check → learn-blocking-clause until either a Boolean model survives the theory check (SAT) or the abstraction itself becomes unsatisfiable (UNSAT).
